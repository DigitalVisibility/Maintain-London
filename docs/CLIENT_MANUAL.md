# Your Project Portal — Customer Guide

> **For:** Customers having building work done
> **Reading time:** About 10 minutes
> **Updated:** July 2026

Welcome! Your builder uses an online portal to keep you up to date on your job. It
is a simple website. This guide shows you how to use it. It is written in easy
steps. You do not need to be good with computers.

In your portal you can:

- **See progress** — updates and photos of your job.
- **Approve extra work** — say yes or no to changes, with one tap.
- **See your money** — the contract total, what you've paid, and what's left.
- **Get your invoices and receipts** — as proper PDFs you can save.
- **See the plan** — the schedule of work.
- **Message your builder** — all in one place.

**Good to know:** You only ever see **your own job**. You cannot see your builder's
other customers or other jobs, and they cannot see yours. Your information is
private.

## 1. Getting in the first time

Your builder will send you an **invite email**.

1. Open the email. It comes from your builder's name.
2. Click the button or link inside it.
3. Choose a **password**. Pick something you'll remember.
4. Click to finish.

You are now in your portal. That's it — you're set up.

> Can't find the email? Check your spam or junk folder. If it's still not there,
> ask your builder to send it again.

---

## 2. Logging in after that

1. Go to **https://maintainlondon.co.uk/project-hub/**
2. Type your email and the password you chose.
3. Click **Sign in**.

You'll land on a page showing your job. If you have more than one job with the same
builder, you'll see them listed — click the one you want.

---

## 3. Your project page

This is your main page. From the top down, you may see:

- A yellow bar if something **needs your approval**.
- **Financial summary** — the big numbers for your job.
- **Daily updates** — progress with photos.
- **Project schedule** — the plan of work.
- **Progress reports** — saved copies of updates sent to you.
- **Documents** — files your builder has shared.
- **Approvals** — extra work to say yes or no to.
- **Messages** — chat with your builder.

You won't always see every section. They appear when there's something in them.

---

## 4. Progress updates and photos

Under **Daily updates** you'll see the days your builder has shared.

- Each day shows a few **photos** of the work.
- Click **View summary** to read a full write-up of that day.

Your builder may also email you a **weekly update**. These are saved under
**Progress reports** so you can read them again any time. Click **Read** to open one.

---

## 5. Approving extra work

Sometimes builders find extra work — for example, hidden damage — or you ask for a
change. This is called a **variation**. It changes the price, so your builder asks
you to approve it.

**The easy way — by email:**

1. You get an email about the extra work, with the price.
2. It has two buttons: **Approve** and **Decline**.
3. Tap the one you want. You don't even need to log in.

**Or in your portal:**

1. Look for the yellow bar that says *"X variations awaiting your approval"*, or
   scroll to **Approvals**.
2. Read the work and the price.
3. Tap **Approve** or **Decline**.

When you approve, the price is added to your job. When you decline, nothing changes.

> **Take your time.** Nothing is charged until you approve it. If you're not sure,
> use **Messages** to ask your builder a question first.

## 6. Your money and invoices

The **Financial summary** at the top shows four simple numbers:

- **Contract sum** — the full agreed price for the job (including any extra work
  you approved).
- **Work complete** — how far along the job is, as a percentage.
- **Paid to date** — how much you've paid so far.
- **Balance outstanding** — how much is still to pay.

Below that is a list of your **invoices**:

- **Paid** invoices are the ones you've already settled.
- **Due** invoices are ones to pay now.

For each invoice you can click:

- **Invoice** — opens a proper PDF invoice. To save it, click **Save as PDF /
  Print** at the top.
- **Receipt** — once an invoice is paid, this opens a receipt for your records.

**How to pay:** the invoice shows your builder's bank details and payment terms.
Pay the way you normally would (usually a bank transfer). The portal does not take
card payments.

---

## 7. The plan (schedule)

Under **Project schedule** you can see the plan of work — each stage, with dates,
shown as a simple bar chart.

- **Grey** means not started.
- **Green** means done.
- The coloured bar shows in-progress work.

This helps you see what's happening now and what's coming next.

---

## 8. Documents

Under **Documents** you'll find files your builder has shared with you — things
like your contract, drawings, or a handover pack.

1. Click a folder to open it.
2. Click a file to open or download it.

You only see files your builder chose to share. Some files are kept internal to
their team.

---

## 9. Messaging your builder

The **Messages** section is a simple chat, just for your job.

1. Scroll to **Messages**.
2. Type your message.
3. Click **Send**.

Your builder gets an email when you write, so they'll see it quickly. Their replies
appear here too. Keeping everything in one place means nothing gets lost in email.

---

## 10. Put it on your phone

You can make the portal open like an app.

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open the portal in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open the portal in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three dots** menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**.

Now it's one tap away, like any app.

## 11. Common questions

**Is my information private?**
Yes. You only see your own job. Your builder's other customers cannot see anything
about you or your job, and you cannot see theirs.

**Do I have to approve extra work straight away?**
No. Take your time. Nothing is charged until you approve. Ask a question in
Messages if you're unsure.

**How do I pay an invoice?**
Open the invoice (click **Invoice**). It shows the bank details and terms. Pay by
bank transfer as normal. The portal doesn't take card payments.

**I approved something by mistake.**
Message your builder straight away. They can sort it out.

**I didn't get an email I was expecting.**
Check your spam or junk folder. Everything is also in your portal, so you can log
in and see it there.

**I forgot my password.**
Use the "forgot password" option on the sign-in page, or ask your builder to help.
